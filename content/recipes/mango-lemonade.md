## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 mango, chopped
- [ ] 1 cup water
- [ ] Sugar, to taste
- [ ] 1 to 2 tbsp lemon juice

## Instructions
- [ ] Blend the mango and water until smooth.
- [ ] Strain through a juice bag or sieve if you want a smoother finish.
- [ ] Add sugar and lemon juice to taste.
- [ ] Serve chilled.
