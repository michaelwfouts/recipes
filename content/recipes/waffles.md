## Ingredients
- [ ] 2 large eggs
- [ ] 2 cups all-purpose flour
- [ ] 1 3/4 cups milk
- [ ] 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- [ ] 1 tbsp white sugar
- [ ] 4 tsp baking powder
- [ ] 1/4 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- [ ] Nonstick cooking spray

## Instructions
- [ ] Preheat a waffle iron according to the manufacturer’s instructions.
- [ ] Whisk the eggs in a large bowl until light and fluffy.
- [ ] Add the flour, milk, and vegetable oil and mix to combine.
- [ ] Whisk in the sugar, then mix in the baking powder, salt, and vanilla until smooth.
- [ ] Spray the waffle iron with nonstick spray.
- [ ] Pour batter into the hot iron and cook until golden brown and the iron stops steaming, about 3 to 5 minutes.
