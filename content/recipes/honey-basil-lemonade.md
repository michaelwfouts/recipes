## Ingredients
- [ ] 3 cups fresh basil leaves
- [ ] 4 cups water
- [ ] 2 cups sugar
- [ ] 1/2 cup lemon juice

## Instructions
- [ ] Lightly blend the basil, sugar, lemon juice, and water.
- [ ] Serve chilled.

## Notes
- Make sure to add the acid to prevent the basil from oxidizing.
- Lightly blend to prevent bitter chlorophyll from being present in the drink.
