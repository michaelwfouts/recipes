## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 mango, peeled and chopped
- [ ] 1/2 cup plain yogurt
- [ ] 1/2 cup milk
- [ ] 1 to 2 tbsp sugar, to taste
- [ ] 1/4 tsp allspice

## Instructions
- [ ] Blend the mango, yogurt, milk, sugar, and allspice until smooth.
- [ ] Taste and adjust the sweetness and spice.
