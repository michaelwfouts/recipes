## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 cucumber, skin removed
- [ ] 1 handful fresh mint
- [ ] 2 cups water
- [ ] lemon juice, to taste
- [ ] Sugar, to taste

## Instructions
- [ ] Muddle or lightly blend the cucumber and mint with a little water.
- [ ] Add the remaining water and stir well.
- [ ] Adjust the flavor with lemon juice and sugar as needed.
- [ ] Serve chilled.

## Notes
- The original notes suggest muddling for a brighter, fresher result.
- A few reserved cucumber slices make an attractive garnish.
