## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 oz simple syrup
- [ ] 1/2 oz fresh lemon juice
- [ ] 1/2 oz fresh lime juice
- [ ] 1 oz pasteurized egg white
- [ ] 2 oz heavy cream
- [ ] Dash of orange blossom water
- [ ] Dash of vanilla extract
- [ ] Club soda, to top

## Instructions
- [ ] Shake or blend the simple syrup, citrus juices, egg white, cream, and vanilla until frothy.
- [ ] Pour into a Collins glass top with club soda.
