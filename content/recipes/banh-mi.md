## Ingredients

- [ ] [Vietnamese Chicken Thighs](#/recipe/vietnamese-chicken-thighs)
- [ ] 1 large carrot, cut into 2-inch matchsticks 1/8-inch-thick (about 2 cups)
- [ ] 1 small daikon radish, cut into 2-inch matchsticks 1/8-inch-thick (about 2 cups)
- [ ] 3/4 cup plus 2 teaspoons sugar
- [ ] Kosher salt
- [ ] 1/2 cup white vinegar
- [ ] 4 Vietnamese baguettes or high-quality hero rolls
- [ ] 4 tablespoons mayonnaise
- [ ] 1 cucumber, peeled, seeded, and cut lengthwise into 8 strips
- [ ] 1 jalapeño pepper, stemmed, seeded, and cut lengthwise into matchsticks
- [ ] 1 bunch fresh cilantro
- [ ] Freshly ground black pepper

## Instructions
- [ ] Place carrot, daikon, 1/4 cup of sugar, and 1 tablespoon of salt in a medium mixing bowl. Using your hands, massage salt and sugar into vegetables until they begin to soften and exude liquid. Set the bowl aside at room temperature for 30 minutes, then drain the vegetables. 
- [ ] Combine the vinegar, 1/2 cup of sugar, and 1/2 cup of water in a small bowl or Tupperware container and mix until sugar is dissolved. Pour vinegar mixture over drained vegetables, and refrigerate until ready to use, at least 1 hour.
- [ ] Split the baguettes and remove the center of the baguettes to allow additional room for sandwich fillings. Place baguettes on a baking sheet and toast for 3 minutes until exterior is crispy. 
- [ ] Serve ingredients as a build your own sandwich bar.
