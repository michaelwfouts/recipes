## Ingredients
- [ ] Watermelon, cubed
- [ ] Sugar, to taste
- [ ] Acid, such as lemon or lime juice
- [ ] Fresh mint leaves

## Instructions
- [ ] Blend watermelon, sugar, and acid.
- [ ] Filter through nut bag for smoothness.
- [ ] Muddle mint into juice.
- [ ] Serve chilled.

## Notes
- Can lightly blend the mint into the drink, but hard to judge strength and can incorporate bitter aftertaste if blended too much.
