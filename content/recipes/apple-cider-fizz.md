## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 to 2 oz apple cider
- [ ] 1 oz simple syrup
- [ ] 1 oz fresh lemon juice
- [ ] 1 fresh egg white
- [ ] 2 oz heavy cream
- [ ] 1 pinch cinnamon
- [ ] 1 dash vanilla extract
- [ ] Club soda, to top

## Instructions
- [ ] Blend the apple cider, simple syrup, lemon juice, egg white, cream, cinnamon, and vanilla until frothy.
- [ ] Pour into a glass over ice and top with club soda.
- [ ] Stir gently and serve immediately.
