## Ingredients
- [ ] 3 lb beef chuck, cut into 2-inch pieces
- [ ] Kosher salt
- [ ] Freshly ground black pepper
- [ ] 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- [ ] 2 tbsp butter
- [ ] 1 2/3 cups dry red wine
- [ ] 3 tbsp tomato paste
- [ ] 2 Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into 1-inch cubes
- [ ] 3 carrots, chopped into 1-inch pieces
- [ ] 3 celery stalks, chopped into 1-inch pieces
- [ ] 2 onions, chopped into quarters
- [ ] 1 cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes
- [ ] 4 cloves garlic, minced
- [ ] 1 large rosemary sprig
- [ ] 2 cups low-sodium beef broth
- [ ] 1 can crushed tomatoes
- [ ] Freshly chopped parsley, for garnish

## Instructions
- [ ] Pat the beef dry and season generously with salt and pepper.
- [ ] Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and sear the beef until well browned.
- [ ] Transfer the beef to a slow cooker.
- [ ] Add butter to the skillet, loosen the browned bits, and stir in the red wine and tomato paste.
- [ ] Simmer for 1 minute and transfer the mixture to the slow cooker.
- [ ] Add the potatoes, carrots, celery, onions, sun-dried tomatoes, garlic, rosemary, beef broth, and crushed tomatoes.
- [ ] Season with more salt if needed and cook on high until the meat is tender, about 4 to 5 hours.
- [ ] Remove the rosemary sprig before serving.
- [ ] Garnish with parsley and serve.
