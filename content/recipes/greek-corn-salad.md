## Ingredients
- [ ] 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- [ ] 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- [ ] 3 tbsp chopped fresh oregano and/or mint
- [ ] 1 tsp honey
- [ ] Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- [ ] 1 English cucumber, peeled, seeded, and chopped
- [ ] 1 pint grape or cherry tomatoes, halved
- [ ] 1 small red onion, diced
- [ ] 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese
- [ ] 2 ears of corn, shucked

## Instructions
- [ ] Dice and salt the cucumber for at least 15 minutes.  Drain and pat dry.
- [ ] Whisk together the olive oil, red wine vinegar, herbs, honey, salt, and pepper.
- [ ] Reserve a small amount of vinaigrette in a large bowl, then toss the cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, and feta with the remaining vinaigrette.
- [ ] Grill the corn until lightly charred, about 8 minutes, turning occasionally.
- [ ] Cut the kernels off the cobs and add them to the salad.
- [ ] Toss the salad again and serve immediately.
