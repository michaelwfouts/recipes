## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 cups chopped pineapple, peeled and chopped
- [ ] 2 cups cucumbers, peeled and chopped
- [ ] 4 cups cold water
- [ ] 1/2 cup sugar, to taste
- [ ] 2 tbsp lime juice or another acid, optional

## Instructions
- [ ] Blend the pineapple, cucumber, sugar with a little water until smooth.
- [ ] Strain through a fine-mesh sieve or nut-milk bag if you want a smoother drink.
- [ ] Stir in the remaining water and sugar until well combined.
- [ ] Add a little lime juice or acid if you want more brightness.
- [ ] Serve over ice and enjoy cold.

## Notes
- [ ] The source notes suggested a ratio of 1 pineapple to 2 cucumbers.
- [ ] This is a very fresh, lightly sweet drink best served chilled.
