## Ingredients
- [ ] 3 tea bags
- [ ] 32 oz water
- [ ] Sugar, to taste
- [ ] 1 squeeze lemon juice, optional

## Instructions
- [ ] Steep the tea bags in water and place in fridge for 24 hours.
- [ ] Sweeten to taste if you want sweet tea.
- [ ] Add lemon juice if desired and serve over ice.
