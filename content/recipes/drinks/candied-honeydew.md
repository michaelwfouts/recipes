## Ingredients
- [ ] Honeydew
- [ ] Sugar
- [ ] Lemon juice

## Instructions
- [ ] Blend the honeydew and strain through a nut milk bag.
- [ ] Combine the honeydew juice, sugar, and acid.
- [ ] Chill and serve.

## Notes
- Optional rim glass with citric acid/sugar mixture
