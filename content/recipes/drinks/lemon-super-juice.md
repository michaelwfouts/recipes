## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 g citric acid
- [ ] 1 g lemon zest
- [ ] 20 g water

## Instructions
- [ ] Zest lemons and weigh mass on scale.
- [ ] Combine citirc acid, lemon zest, and water in a ratio of 1:1:20.
