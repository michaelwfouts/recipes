## Ingredients
- [ ] 1/2 cup almond flour
- [ ] 2 cups milk
- [ ] 1 pinch Saffron
- [ ] 1/8 tsp. cardamom powder
- [ ] 2 tsp. sugar
- [ ] Cinnamon, to taste
- [ ] Vanilla extract, optional

## Instructions
- [ ] Blend the almond flour, milk, saffron, cardamom, sugar, cinnamon, and vanilla.
- [ ] Strain through a nut milk bag for smoother texture.
- [ ] Chill and serve.
