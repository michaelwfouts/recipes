## Ingredients
- [ ] 3 to 5 dates, pitted
- [ ] 1 glass milk
- [ ] 2-6 dashes of cinnamon, to taste
- [ ] 1-2 splashes of vanilla extract, to taste
- [ ] Sugar, optional

## Instructions
- [ ] Blend three dates, milk, cinnamon, and vanilla until smooth.
- [ ] Taste and adjust with more sugar/dates if needed.
- [ ] Chill and serve.
