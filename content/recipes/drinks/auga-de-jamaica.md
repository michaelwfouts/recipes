## Ingredients
- [ ] 8 cups water
- [ ] 1 cup dried hibiscus petals
- [ ] 2 cups sugar

## Instructions
- [ ] Bring the water, hibiscus petals, and sugar to a boil for 5 minutes.
- [ ] Remove from heat and let the mixture cool to room temperature.
- [ ] Strain out the hibiscus petals and chill before serving.
