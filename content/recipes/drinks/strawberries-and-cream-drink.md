## Ingredients
- [ ] Fresh strawberries
- [ ] Sugar
- [ ] Acid, such as lemon juice
- [ ] Milk or heavy cream
- [ ] whipped cream, optional

## Instructions
- [ ] Blend the strawberries, sugar, acid, and dairy until smooth.
- [ ] Taste and adjust with more sugar or acid.
- [ ] Garnish with mint if desired.
