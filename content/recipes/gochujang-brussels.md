## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 lb Brussels sprouts, halved
- [ ] 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- [ ] 1 tbsp gochujang
- [ ] 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- [ ] Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Instructions
- [ ] Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the Brussels sprouts cut-side down with a pinch of salt and cook with a lid on until the edges are browned.
- [ ] Remove the lid and continue cooking for 2 minutes more to let the surface crisp.
- [ ] Stir in the gochujang and rice vinegar until the Brussels are evenly coated.
- [ ] Cook for 1 minute more, then remove from the heat and serve.
