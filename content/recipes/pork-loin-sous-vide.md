## Ingredients
- [ ] Pork tenderloin or pork loin steaks
- [ ] Salt and black pepper, to taste
- [ ] Fresh herbs or bay leaves, optional
- [ ] White vinegar
- [ ] Water
- [ ] Brown sugar
- [ ] Garlic, minced

## Instructions
- [ ] Season the pork with salt and pepper. Add any fresh herbs or bay leaves you like inside the sous-vide bag.
- [ ] Cook the pork in a sous-vide bath at 130°F for juicy medium-rare doneness, or 135°F if you want it more fully cooked. Cook for 1 to 4 hours depending on thickness.
- [ ] Refrigerate until ready to serve if needed.
- [ ] Before serving, sear the pork in a hot skillet until nicely browned.
- [ ] Make a quick pan sauce by simmering white vinegar, water, brown sugar, and garlic until glossy and reduced.
