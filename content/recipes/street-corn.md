## Ingredients
- [ ] 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- [ ] 4 ears fresh corn, kernels removed
- [ ] Kosher salt, to taste
- [ ] 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- [ ] 2 oz feta or Cotija cheese, finely crumbled
- [ ] 1/2 cup finely sliced scallions, green parts only
- [ ] 1/2 cup fresh cilantro leaves, finely chopped
- [ ] 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and stemmed, finely chopped
- [ ] 1 to 2 cloves garlic, pressed or minced
- [ ] 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
- [ ] Chili powder or hot chili flakes, to taste

## Instructions
- [ ] Heat the oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat until shimmering.
- [ ] Add the corn kernels, season with salt, and cook without moving until charred on one side, about 2 minutes.
- [ ] Toss and char the corn on the second side for another 2 minutes. Continue tossing and charring until the corn is well charred all over, about 10 minutes total.
- [ ] Transfer the corn to a bowl and add the mayonnaise, cheese, scallions, cilantro, jalapeño, garlic, lime juice, and chili powder.
- [ ] Toss to combine and taste for seasoning. Adjust with salt and more chili powder if needed.
