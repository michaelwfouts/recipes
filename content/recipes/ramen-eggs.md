## Ingredients
- [ ] 4 large eggs
- [ ] 1/4 cup soy sauce
- [ ] 1/4 cup mirin
- [ ] 1/4 cup sake (or water)
- [ ] 1 tsp sugar

## Instructions
- [ ] Soft boil the eggs, about 7 minutes in a boiling pot of water.
- [ ] Combine the soy sauce, mirin, sake, and sugar for the marinade.
- [ ] Peel the eggs and place in the marinade for a minimum of 12 hours and maximum of 5 days.
