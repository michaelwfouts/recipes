## Ingredients

### Chicken
- [ ] 6 medium garlic cloves
- [ ] 1/4 cup freshly squeezed orange juice
- [ ] 1/4 cup freshly squeezed lime juice
- [ ] 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] 1 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- [ ] 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- [ ] 1/2 tsp ground allspice, optional
- [ ] Kosher salt, to taste
- [ ] 4 boneless, skinless chicken breast halves or 8 chicken cutlets
- [ ] 1 tbsp neutral oil, plus more as needed
- [ ] 1 large white or yellow onion, sliced into 1/4-inch rings

### Serve With
- [ ] Cooked long-grain rice, for serving
- [ ] Black beans, for serving
- [ ] Picked cilantro leaves, for serving
- [ ] Lime wedges, for serving

## Instructions
- [ ] In a blender or food processor, purée the garlic, orange juice, lime juice, cumin, oregano, black pepper, allspice if using, and 2 tsp kosher salt until smooth.
- [ ] If using chicken breast halves, cut them into cutlets and pound to an even thickness of about 1/4 inch if needed.
- [ ] Add the chicken to a zipper bag or shallow dish and coat with the marinade. Refrigerate for 30 minutes.
- [ ] Remove the chicken from the marinade and wipe off excess marinade.
- [ ] Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken in batches until golden brown on each side, 2 to 3 minutes per side. Transfer to a platter and keep warm.
- [ ] In the same skillet, add a little more oil if needed and cook the onion rings with a pinch of salt until softened and lightly browned, 7 to 10 minutes.
- [ ] Arrange the chicken on serving plates and top with the browned onions.
- [ ] Serve with rice, black beans, cilantro, and lime wedges.

## Notes
- Do not marinate the chicken for much longer than 30 minutes or the acid may affect the texture.
