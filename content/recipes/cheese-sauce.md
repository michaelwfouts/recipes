## Ingredients
- [ ] 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- [ ] 6 cloves garlic, grated or finely minced
- [ ] 2 tbsp flour
- [ ] 1 1/2 cups half-and-half or heavy cream
- [ ] 1/2 cup chicken stock or vegetable stock or water
- [ ] 2 tbsp fresh parsley, minced
- [ ] Kosher salt and fresh black pepper, to taste
- [ ] Leftover cheese, to taste

## Instructions
- [ ] Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
- [ ] Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
- [ ] Stir in the flour and cook until lightly golden, 1 to 2 minutes.
- [ ] Slowly whisk in the half-and-half and stock.
- [ ] Simmer until the sauce thickens, 1 to 2 minutes.
- [ ] Stir in the parsley and season with salt and pepper.
- [ ] Add shredded or leftover cheese to taste, if using.

## Notes
- This is a quick, flexible cheese sauce meant to use up extra cheese or cream.
