## Ingredients
- [ ] 8 garlic cloves, peeled and crushed
- [ ] 1 2-inch piece ginger, peeled and sliced
- [ ] 1/2 cup dry sake
- [ ] 1/2 cup gochujang
- [ ] 1/2 cup mirin
- [ ] 1/4 cup vegetable oil, plus extra for grilling
- [ ] 1 1/2 lb skinless, boneless pork shoulder, sliced 3/4 inch thick

## Instructions
- [ ] Purée the garlic, ginger, sake, gochujang, mirin, and 1/4 cup vegetable oil in a blender until smooth.
- [ ] Reserve 1/4 cup of the marinade in the refrigerator. Toss the pork in the remaining marinade and chill, turning occasionally, for at least 2 hours.
- [ ] Prepare the grill for medium-high heat and oil the grate.
- [ ] Remove the pork from the marinade and grill, basting with the reserved marinade, turning occasionally, until cooked to your preferred doneness, about 8 to 10 minutes for medium-rare.
- [ ] Let the pork rest for 5 minutes, then slice thinly against the grain.
