## Ingredients
- [ ] 3 large russet potatoes
- [ ] Canola oil, for deep frying
- [ ] 1 to 2 tsp Cajun seasoning, to taste
- [ ] 1 to 2 tsp Salt, to taste
- [ ] 1 to 2 tsp Garlic powder, to taste

## Instructions
- [ ] Wash the potatoes and cut them into fries.
- [ ] Rinse the potatoes and soak them in cold water for at least 20 minutes to remove excess starch and improve crispness.
- [ ] Heat a pot of canola oil over high heat.
- [ ] Drain the potatoes and pat them very dry with paper towels.
- [ ] Fry the potatoes in batches until golden brown and crisp.
- [ ] Remove the fries and season immediately with a mixture of Cajun seasoning, garlic powder, and salt to taste.
- [ ] Blot off excess oil on paper towels and serve hot.
