## Ingredients
- [ ] 14 cups crusty bread, cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- [ ] 8 tbsp unsalted butter
- [ ] 8 oz chorizo, diced
- [ ] 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
- [ ] 1 sprig rosemary
- [ ] 2 cups onion, diced fine
- [ ] 6 cloves garlic, minced
- [ ] 4 cups chicken or turkey stock
- [ ] Pinch saffron
- [ ] 1 tbsp sugar, to taste

## Instructions
- [ ] Preheat the oven to 275°F. Spread the bread cubes on a baking sheet and bake until dry, about 45 minutes. Transfer to a large bowl.
- [ ] Increase the oven temperature to 350°F.
- [ ] Heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add the butter and chorizo and cook until the sausage is crisp and the fat turns orange-red.
- [ ] Stir in the paprika, rosemary, and onions and cook until the onions are translucent. Add the garlic and olives and cook for 1 minute more.
- [ ] Add the stock and saffron to the skillet and bring to a bare simmer. Cook for 5 minutes to combine the flavors. Taste and adjust with salt and sugar as needed.
- [ ] Remove the rosemary sprig.
- [ ] Pour the hot broth mixture over the bread cubes and toss until well combined.
- [ ] Transfer the stuffing to a 9-by-13-inch baking dish and spread out evenly; do not pack it down.
- [ ] Bake for 30 minutes, or until the top is well browned and the center is still moist.
