## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 tbsp oil
- [ ] 1/2 large yellow onion, finely chopped
- [ ] 4 cloves garlic, minced
- [ ] 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut into 3/4-inch pieces
- [ ] 2 tbsp soy sauce
- [ ] 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
- [ ] 5 cups loosely packed baby spinach
- [ ] 1 tbsp lemon zest
- [ ] 2 tbsp freshly squeezed lemon juice
- [ ] 2 cups fresh basil leaves, chiffonade
- [ ] Kosher salt and pepper, to taste
- [ ] Prepared brown rice, for serving
- [ ] 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar, optional but recommended

### Serve With
- [ ] Rice

## Instructions
- [ ] Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
- [ ] Add the onion and cook until softened, about 4 minutes.
- [ ] Stir in the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
- [ ] Add the chicken and cook over medium-high heat until browned on all sides, about 3 minutes.
- [ ] Stir in the soy sauce and black pepper and cook until the chicken is done, about 3 more minutes.
- [ ] Add the spinach in batches and cook until wilted.
- [ ] Stir in the lemon zest, lemon juice, and balsamic vinegar.
- [ ] Remove from the heat and add the basil, stirring just until wilted.
- [ ] Season to taste and serve warm over rice.
