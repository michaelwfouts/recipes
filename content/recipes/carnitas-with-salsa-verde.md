## Ingredients
### Pork
- [ ] 2 medium onions, divided
- [ ] 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
- [ ] 3 lb boneless pork shoulder, cut into 2-inch cubes
- [ ] 1 tbsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- [ ] 1 medium orange
- [ ] 6 cloves garlic, halved, divided
- [ ] 2 bay leaves
- [ ] 1 cinnamon stick, broken into 3 or 4 pieces
- [ ] 1/4 cup vegetable oil

### Salsa Verde
- [ ] 6 medium tomatillos, peeled and halved
- [ ] 2 jalapeño peppers, stemmed and halved lengthwise
- [ ] 2 onion quarters, reserved from above
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves, reserved from above
- [ ] Water, as needed

### To Serve
- [ ] 24 corn tortillas
- [ ] 1 cup crumbled queso fresco or feta
- [ ] 3 limes, cut into wedges

## Instructions
- [ ] Adjust the oven rack to the middle position and preheat the oven to 275°F. Cut one onion into a fine dice and combine with the cilantro. Refrigerate until needed. Split the remaining onion into quarters and set aside.
- [ ] Season the pork with the kosher salt and place it in a 9-by-13-inch glass baking dish. Squeeze the orange juice over the pork and nestle the squeezed orange pieces into the dish. Add the onion quarters, 4 cloves of garlic, bay leaves, and cinnamon stick. Pour over the vegetable oil and cover tightly with foil.
- [ ] Roast until the pork is fork-tender, about 3 1/2 hours.
- [ ] Set a fine-mesh strainer over a bowl. Remove and discard the orange peel, onion, garlic, cinnamon stick, and bay leaves. Transfer the pork, rendered fat, and cooking liquid to the strainer and let drain for 10 minutes.
- [ ] Transfer the pork back to the baking dish; skim off some fat and add it back to the pork if needed. Shred the pork into large chunks with fingers or forks.
- [ ] Simmer the tomatillos, remaining onion quarters, remaining garlic cloves, and jalapeños in the strained pork liquid, adding water until the vegetables sit about 1 inch below the surface. Cook until all the vegetables are tender, about 10 minutes.
- [ ] Blend the salsa until smooth with an immersion blender or countertop blender, then season to taste with salt. Cool and refrigerate until ready to serve.
- [ ] To crisp the pork, broil it on a sheet pan until browned and crisp on the surface, about 6 minutes, then stir and broil again until crisp, 6 more minutes.
- [ ] Warm the tortillas in a skillet, then stack two tortillas per serving. Add the carnitas, then top with salsa verde, chopped onion-cilantro mixture, queso fresco, and lime wedges.
