## Ingredients
- [ ] 8 cloves of garlic
- [ ] 1 tablespoon ginger paste
- [ ] 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- [ ] 2 tablespoons vinegar or lemon juice
- [ ] 16 oz spinach

## Instructions
- [ ] Sauté ginger and garlic until fragrant in a large pot.
- [ ] Add soy sauce and acid to deglaze any aromatics.
- [ ] Add spinach and toss until wilted and coated in liquid.
