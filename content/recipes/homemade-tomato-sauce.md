## Ingredients
- [ ] 4 to 5 tomatoes
- [ ] 1 onion
- [ ] 3 cloves garlic
- [ ] Lime juice or another acid, to taste
- [ ] Salt, to taste
- [ ] 2 tbsp butter
- [ ] Sriracha or another hot sauce, to taste

## Instructions
- [ ] Roast the tomatoes, onion, and garlic until softened and lightly charred.
- [ ] Transfer the roasted vegetables to a blender and blend until smooth.
- [ ] Add acid, salt, butter, and a drizzle of sriracha to brighten and balance the sauce. Adjust to taste.
- [ ] Adjust the seasoning to taste and serve warm.
