## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 cup applesauce
- [ ] 1 cup milk
- [ ] 1/4 tsp allspice
- [ ] 2 to 3 tbsp sugar, to taste
- [ ] 1 tbsp lemon juice
- [ ] Graham crackers, optional

## Instructions
- [ ] Blend the applesauce, milk, allspice, sugar, and lemon juice until smooth.
- [ ] Taste and adjust the sweetness or acidity.
- [ ] Serve chilled with Graham crackers sprinkled on top.
