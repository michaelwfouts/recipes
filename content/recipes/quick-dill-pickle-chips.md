## Ingredients
- [ ] 2 Kirby cucumbers, thinly sliced
- [ ] 1 cup water
- [ ] 1 cup distilled white vinegar
- [ ] 1 tbsp kosher salt
- [ ] Pinch red pepper flakes, optional
- [ ] 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- [ ] 2 tsp black peppercorns
- [ ] 2 tsp yellow mustard seed
- [ ] 2 sprigs fresh dill

## Instructions
- [ ] Place the cucumber slices in a bowl that just fits them.
- [ ] Heat the water, vinegar, salt, pepper flakes, garlic, peppercorns, mustard seed, and dill in a skillet over high heat until the salt dissolves and the mixture is simmering.
- [ ] Immediately pour the hot brine over the cucumbers.
- [ ] Cover the bowl with a paper towel pressed against the surface of the liquid to keep the cucumbers submerged.
- [ ] Let stand for at least 30 minutes before serving. They can be stored in the brine in the refrigerator for up to 1 month.
