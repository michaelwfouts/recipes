## Ingredients
- [ ] 1/2 cup mayo
- [ ] 1 tablespoon hot sauce (Frank's)
- [ ] 1 teaspoon soy sauce
- [ ] 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- [ ] 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder

## Instructions
- [ ] Whisk the mayo, hot sauce, soy sauce, sesame oil, and garlic powder together in a bowl until smooth.
