## Ingredients
### Broth
- [ ] 7 cloves garlic, finely minced
- [ ] 1 3-inch knob ginger, grated
- [ ] 2 medium shallots, finely diced
- [ ] 1/2 lb ground pork
- [ ] 4 tbsp miso
- [ ] 1.5 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted and ground
- [ ] 1 tbsp hondashi, optional
- [ ] 1 tbsp sugar
- [ ] 5.5 cups chicken stock
- [ ] Salt, to taste
- [ ] 1 container noodles

### Toppings
- [ ] 4 eggs
- [ ] 1 green onion, thinly sliced
- [ ] 1/2 cup corn
- [ ] 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- [ ] Nori, cut into pieces
- [ ] Sesame seeds, optional
- [ ] Chili oil, optional

## Instructions
- [ ] Heat a pot over medium-high heat and sear the ground pork in it until browned.
- [ ] Break the pork into small pieces and cook until fully rendered.
- [ ] Add the shallots, garlic, and ginger and cook until fragrant.
- [ ] Stir in the sugar and cook until dissolved.
- [ ] Return the pork to the pot and stir in the sesame seeds and miso.
- [ ] Add the chicken stock and optional hondashi, then simmer gently.
- [ ] Cook the noodles according to the package instructions.
- [ ] Build bowls with noodles, hot broth, corn, green onion, egg, sesame seeds, and nori.
