## Ingredients
- [ ] 2 4-oz bars dark baking chocolate
- [ ] 1 cup cocoa powder
- [ ] 2 cups sugar
- [ ] 2 tbsp cornstarch
- [ ] 1/2 tsp kosher salt

## Instructions
- [ ] Combine the chocolate, cocoa powder, sugar, cornstarch, and salt.
- [ ] Use 2 tbsp of the mix per 1 cup oat milk when preparing hot chocolate.  You can use the microwave or stove top.
