## Ingredients
- [ ] 6 anchovy fillets packed in oil, drained
- [ ] 1 small garlic clove
- [ ] Kosher salt
- [ ] 2 large egg yolks
- [ ] 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice, plus more
- [ ] 3/4 tsp Dijon mustard
- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- [ ] 3 tbsp finely grated Parmesan
- [ ] Freshly ground black pepper

## Instructions
- [ ] Mince the garlic and mash it with a pinch of salt.
- [ ] Whisk in the egg yolks, lemon juice, and Dijon until smooth.
- [ ] Slowly whisk in the olive oil and vegetable oil until emulsified.
- [ ] Stir in the grated Parmesan and season with black pepper to taste.
