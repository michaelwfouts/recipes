## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 red onion, thinly sliced from root to stem
- [ ] Equal parts water and vinegar (about 1 cup)

## Instructions
- [ ] Slice the red onion thinly, root to stem.
- [ ] Bring vinegar and water to a boil.
- [ ] Place over onions and let cool for at least 15 minutes.
- [ ] Store in fridge for up to one week.
