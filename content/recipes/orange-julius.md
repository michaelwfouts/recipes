## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 cup fresh squeezed orange juice
- [ ] 1 cup milk
- [ ] Vanilla extract
- [ ] Sweetener, to taste

## Instructions
- [ ] Blend the orange juice, milk, vanilla, and sweetener until smooth.
- [ ] Serve over ice or chilled.
