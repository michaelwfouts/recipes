## Ingredients
- [ ] 3 Cans (5 oz) of Chicken
- [ ] 2 Blocks (8 oz) of Cream Cheese
- [ ] Ranch Dressing
- [ ] Hot Sauce (Franks preferred)
- [ ] Garlic powder
- [ ] Shredded cheese (sharp cheddar preferred)

## Instructions
- [ ] Mix ingredients together. Add Ranch until desired consistency and hot sauce for spice level.

## Notes
- I used lite ranch and low cal cream cheese to make this less calorie intense
