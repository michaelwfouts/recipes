## Ingredients
- [ ] 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- [ ] 1 medium onion, diced
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 2 tsp freshly grated ginger
- [ ] 3 tsp garam masala
- [ ] 2 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] 1 tsp ground turmeric
- [ ] 1 tsp sweet paprika
- [ ] 1 tsp mustard seeds
- [ ] 2 lb russet potatoes, scrubbed and cut into 1-inch pieces
- [ ] 2 cups vegetable or chicken broth
- [ ] 1 can full-fat coconut milk
- [ ] 1 can diced tomatoes
- [ ] 2 cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- [ ] Salt and pepper, to taste
- [ ] 2 tbsp cornstarch mixed with cold water, optional for thicker sauce

## Instructions
- [ ] Coat a large pot with oil and heat over medium heat.
- [ ] Add the onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
- [ ] Stir in the garlic and ginger until fragrant.
- [ ] Add the garam masala, cumin, turmeric, paprika, and mustard seeds and cook for about 1 minute.
- [ ] Stir in the potatoes, broth, and coconut milk.
- [ ] Raise the heat and bring the liquid to a boil, then simmer until the potatoes are tender.
- [ ] Stir in the tomatoes and chickpeas and simmer uncovered until the sauce thickens and the potatoes are very tender.
- [ ] If needed, add cornstarch slurry to reach the desired thickness.
- [ ] Season with salt and pepper and serve with fresh cilantro.
