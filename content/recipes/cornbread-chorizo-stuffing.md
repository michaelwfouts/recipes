## Ingredients
- [ ] 1/2 cup chorizo
- [ ] 2 cloves garlic, minced
- [ ] 1 onion, diced
- [ ] 1 carrot, peeled and diced
- [ ] 1 stalk celery, diced
- [ ] 2 cups diced jalapeño cornbread
- [ ] 2 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
- [ ] 1/2 cup chicken stock
- [ ] Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

## Instructions
- [ ] Preheat the oven to 350°F and lightly coat an 8x8 baking dish with nonstick spray.
- [ ] Cook the chorizo in a skillet until browned, breaking it into small pieces.
- [ ] Add the garlic, onion, carrot, and celery and cook until the vegetables are browned, about 8 to 10 minutes.
- [ ] Stir in the cornbread, cilantro, and chicken stock, then season with salt and pepper.
- [ ] Transfer the mixture to the prepared dish and bake until heated through and lightly browned on top, about 20 minutes.
- [ ] Serve immediately, garnished with cilantro if desired.
