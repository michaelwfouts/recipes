## Ingredients
- [ ] 1/4 cup honey
- [ ] 2 tbsp cider vinegar
- [ ] 2 tbsp Sriracha sauce
- [ ] 1 tbsp chili powder
- [ ] 1 tsp smoked paprika
- [ ] 1 tsp garlic powder
- [ ] 1 tsp onion powder
- [ ] 1/2 tsp salt
- [ ] 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- [ ] 3/4 cup brown ale
- [ ] 3 tbsp cornstarch
- [ ] 3 tbsp water
- [ ] 12 slider buns

### For Serving
- [ ] Pickles
- [ ] Sriracha

## Instructions
- [ ] In a 3- or 4-quart slow cooker, combine the honey, cider vinegar, Sriracha, chili powder, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, and salt.
- [ ] Add the chicken thighs and brown ale and toss to coat.
- [ ] Cook on low until the chicken is tender, 6 to 8 hours.
- [ ] Remove the chicken and shred it with two forks.
- [ ] Strain the cooking liquid and skim off the fat. Pour the juices into a saucepan and bring to a boil.
- [ ] Stir together the cornstarch and water, then whisk it into the sauce.
- [ ] Cook until thickened, about 5 minutes, then return the shredded chicken to the sauce and toss to coat.
- [ ] Serve on slider buns with pickles and extra Sriracha, if desired.
