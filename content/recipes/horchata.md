## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 cup uncooked white rice
- [ ] 2 cinnamon sticks
- [ ] 1/2 star anise
- [ ] 4 cups warm water
- [ ] 12 oz evaporated milk
- [ ] 12 oz sweetened condensed milk
- [ ] 4 cups additional water, divided
- [ ] Sugar, to taste
- [ ] Vanilla extract, to taste
- [ ] Ground cinnamon, to taste

## Instructions
- [ ] Rinse the rice and soak it in water with the cinnamon sticks and star anise for at least 4 hours or overnight.
- [ ] Blend the rice mixture in batches until smooth.
- [ ] Strain through a fine-mesh sieve or cheesecloth, pressing to extract as much liquid as possible.
- [ ] Stir in the evaporated milk, condensed milk, vanilla, and the remaining water.
- [ ] Add sugar to taste and chill well.
