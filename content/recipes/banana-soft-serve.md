## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 banana, chopped
- [ ] 1 to 2 tbsp sugar, to taste
- [ ] milk or yogurt for optional changes in texture
- [ ] Cinnamon, optional
- [ ] Chocolate chips, optional

## Instructions
- [ ] Freeze the banana pieces for at least 2 hours.
- [ ] Blend the frozen banana with sugar and a little milk until smooth and soft-serve-like.
- [ ] Add cinnamon or chocolate chips if desired.
- [ ] Serve immediately.

## Notes
- Can give a slimey texture with unripe bananas.
