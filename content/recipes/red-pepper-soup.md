## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 Tablespoon Fat (oil, butter, etc. based on preference)
- [ ] 4 stalks of celery, chopped
- [ ] 1 sweet onion, chopped
- [ ] Handful of baby carrots third of bag, chopped
- [ ] 4 red/orange/yellow bell peppers
- [ ] 2/3 container chicken stock (24oz.)
- [ ] Hot sauce of choice

## Instructions
- [ ] With oil or butter, coat the bottom of the pot and sweat the chopped mirepoix (celery, onion, carrots) under low heat until soft. Add salt to help draw out water.
- [ ] Add 4 red orange or yellow peppers and sweat. Salt again.
- [ ] Add two tablespoons of tomato paste and 12 cloves of garlic and cook until fragrant.
- [ ] Cover with chicken stock, about 24 oz. Salt and pepper to taste.
- [ ] Add 3 dashes of hot sauce and blend until desired consistency. Hand blender for rough chopped or blender for a smoother consistency (I prefer the smoother option of the two).

## Notes
- If you want to make it creamy and more like a moqueca, try adding coconut milk next time.
