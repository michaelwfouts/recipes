## Ingredients
### Pork
- [ ] 1 boneless pork butt (about 5 lb), cut in half horizontally
- [ ] 1/2 cup plus 2 tbsp sugar
- [ ] 1 cup plus 2 tsp table salt
- [ ] 1/4 cup yellow mustard
- [ ] 3 tbsp plus 2 tsp liquid smoke
- [ ] 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- [ ] 2 tbsp smoked paprika
- [ ] 2 tbsp ground black pepper

### Sweet and Tangy Barbecue Sauce
- [ ] 1/4 cup light or mild molasses
- [ ] 1 1/2 cups ketchup
- [ ] 1 tbsp hot sauce
- [ ] 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- [ ] 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- [ ] 1/2 tsp table salt

## Instructions
- [ ] Dissolve 3 tbsp of the liquid smoke, 1/2 cup of the sugar, and 1 cup of the salt in 4 quarts of cold water in a large container. Submerge the pork in the brine, cover, and refrigerate for 2 hours.
- [ ] While the pork brines, combine the remaining 2 tsp liquid smoke and the mustard in a small bowl. In a second bowl, combine the paprika, black pepper, cayenne, the remaining 2 tsp salt, and the remaining 2 tbsp sugar.
- [ ] Adjust the oven rack to the lower-middle position and heat the oven to 325°F.
- [ ] Remove the pork from the brine and dry it thoroughly with paper towels. Rub the mustard mixture over the entire surface of each pork piece, then sprinkle with the spice mixture.
- [ ] Place the pork on a wire rack set inside a foil-lined rimmed baking sheet. Cover with parchment and foil, sealing the edges, and roast for 3 hours.
- [ ] Remove the parchment and foil. Pour off the liquid in the baking sheet into a fat separator and reserve it for the sauce. Return the pork to the oven, uncovered, and cook until well browned, tender, and the internal temperature reaches 200°F, about 1 1/2 hours.
- [ ] Transfer the pork to a serving dish, tent loosely with foil, and rest for 20 minutes.
- [ ] While the pork rests, pour 1/2 cup of the defatted cooking liquid into a medium bowl and whisk in the sauce ingredients until smooth.
- [ ] Shred the pork with two forks into bite-size pieces. Toss with 1 cup of the sauce and season with additional pepper and salt. Serve, passing the remaining sauce separately.
