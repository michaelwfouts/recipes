## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 lb green beans, trimmed
- [ ] Salt, to taste
- [ ] 1 tbsp soy sauce
- [ ] 1 tbsp Chinese rice wine or dry sherry
- [ ] 1 tbsp minced garlic
- [ ] 1 tbsp minced fresh ginger
- [ ] 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- [ ] 1 tbsp peanut or canola oil

## Instructions
- [ ] Bring a pot of water to a boil. Salt the water lightly and cook the green beans for 1 minute.
- [ ] Drain the beans and rinse with cold water. Pat them dry thoroughly.
- [ ] In a small bowl, combine the soy sauce and rice wine or sherry.
- [ ] Heat a wok or skillet until very hot. Add the oil and swirl to coat the pan.
- [ ] Add the garlic, ginger, and red pepper flakes and stir-fry for no more than 10 seconds.
- [ ] Add the green beans and toss to coat. Stir in the soy sauce mixture and continue stir-frying until the beans are crisp-tender, 1 to 2 minutes.
- [ ] Remove from the heat and serve.
