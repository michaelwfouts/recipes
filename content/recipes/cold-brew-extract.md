## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 cup coffee grounds
- [ ] 4 cups water

## Instructions
- [ ] Steep the coffee grounds in water for a cold brew concentrate.
- [ ] Double-filter the mixture for a cleaner result.
- [ ] To serve, pour half a cup of concentrate over ice and top with hot water or milk as desired.
