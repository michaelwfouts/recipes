## Ingredients
- [ ] 2 Tbsp. lemongrass paste
- [ ] 1 serrano chili
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves
- [ ] 1 1" piece ginger, peeled
- [ ] 1 Tbsp. dark brown sugar
- [ ] 1 Tbsp. extra-virgin olive oil
- [ ] 1 Tbsp. fish sauce
- [ ] 1 Tbsp. soy sauce
- [ ] 1¼ lb. skinless, boneless chicken thighs
### Optional Toppings
- [ ] 2 scallions, thinly sliced
- [ ] ½ cup cilantro leaves with tender stems
- [ ] Sesame seeds and lime wedges (for serving)

## Instructions
- [ ] Finely chop chili (seeds and all), garlic, and ginger.  Add with brown sugar, oil, fish sauce, and soy sauce into a plastic bag and mix well. Add chicken thighs to the marinade and allow them to marinate from 2 to 24 hours.
- [ ] Place the chicken onto an aluminum foil lined baking pan and cook in a convection oven at 400°F for 10 minutes.
- [ ] Transfer to a platter and let rest for at least 10 minutes. Enjoy with bowl of rice or as a main meat on a sandwich.
