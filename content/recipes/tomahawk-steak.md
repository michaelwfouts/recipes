## Ingredients
### Steak
- [ ] 1 tomahawk steak
- [ ] Salt and pepper, to taste

### Steakhouse Sauce
- [ ] 1/2 cup Worcestershire sauce
- [ ] 1/4 cup soy sauce
- [ ] 3 tbsp brown sugar
- [ ] 4 anchovy fillets
- [ ] 2 cloves garlic
- [ ] 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- [ ] 2 tbsp tomato paste
- [ ] 1/2 cup vegetable oil

## Instructions
- [ ] For medium-rare doneness, cook the tomahawk steak in a sous-vide bath at 130°F.  Sear before serving.
- [ ] Combine the Worcestershire sauce, soy sauce, brown sugar, anchovies, garlic, Dijon, tomato paste, and vegetable oil in a blender and blend until smooth and emulsified.
- [ ] Sear the steak after sous-vide cooking until deeply browned on the outside.
- [ ] Slice and serve with the sauce.
