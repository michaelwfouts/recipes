## Ingredients
- [ ] 2 tsp chili powder
- [ ] 1 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] 1 tsp smoked paprika
- [ ] 1 tsp dried oregano
- [ ] 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- [ ] Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- [ ] 1 1/2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- [ ] 12 mini flour tortillas, warmed
- [ ] 1 cup pico de gallo
- [ ] 1 avocado, diced
- [ ] 1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro
- [ ] 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Instructions
- [ ] In a small bowl, combine the chili powder, cumin, paprika, oregano, garlic powder, 1 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp pepper. Season the chicken with the spice mixture.
- [ ] Add the chicken to lined pan and cook in convection oven at 400°F until internal temperature reaches 165°F.
- [ ] Let the chicken rest briefly, then dice into bite-size pieces.
- [ ] Warm tortillas and serve with the chicken and top with pico de gallo, avocado, cilantro, and lime wedges.
