## Ingredients
### Pecan Brittle
- [ ] 1 1/4 cups coarsely chopped pecans
- [ ] 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- [ ] 4 tbsp unsalted butter
- [ ] 1/2 tsp baking soda
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt or 1/4 tsp Morton kosher salt

### Cookies
- [ ] 1 cup unsalted butter, divided
- [ ] 1 1/3 cups all-purpose flour
- [ ] 2 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt or 1 tsp Morton kosher salt
- [ ] 1 tsp baking soda
- [ ] 2 cups old-fashioned oats, divided
- [ ] 3/4 cup packed dark brown sugar
- [ ] 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- [ ] 2 large eggs
- [ ] 1 tbsp vanilla extract

## Instructions
- [ ] Toast the pecans until slightly darkened and fragrant, then cool.
- [ ] Cook the sugar, butter, and water until it turns deep amber.
- [ ] Stir in the pecans, baking soda, and salt and spread into a thin layer to harden.
- [ ] Chop the brittle into pea-size pieces and set aside.
- [ ] Brown half the butter and cool it slightly.
- [ ] Pulse the flour, salt, baking soda, half the brittle, and 1 cup oats together until finely ground.
- [ ] Beat the browned butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until smooth.
- [ ] Add the eggs and vanilla and mix until light and satiny.
- [ ] Mix in the flour mixture, then fold in the remaining brittle and oats.
- [ ] Chill the dough for at least 12 hours.
- [ ] Scoop the dough onto lined sheets and bake at 350°F until deep golden brown, about 12 to 15 minutes.
- [ ] Cool before serving.
