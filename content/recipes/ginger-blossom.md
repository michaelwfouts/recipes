## Ingredients
- [ ] Ginger discs
- [ ] Drizzle of honey
- [ ] Splash of seltzer
- [ ] Splash of lime juice
- [ ] Chinotto or Aperol with ABV
- [ ] Mint, optional

## Instructions
- [ ] Muddle the ginger with a little honey and lime juice in a glass.
- [ ] Add ice and a splash of seltzer.
- [ ] Pour in Chinotto or Aperol as desired.
- [ ] Stir gently and garnish with mint if using.
