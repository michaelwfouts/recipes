## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 cucumber, sliced or chopped
- [ ] 1 handful fresh mint leaves
- [ ] 4 cups cold water
- [ ] 2 tbsp sugar, to taste
- [ ] 1 tbsp lemon juice, optional

## Instructions
- [ ] Muddle or lightly blend the cucumber and mint with a little water.
- [ ] Add the remaining water and sugar and stir until dissolved.
- [ ] Adjust with lemon juice and more sugar if needed.
- [ ] Serve over ice, and add sparkling water for extra fizz if desired.
