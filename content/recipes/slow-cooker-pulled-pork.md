## Ingredients
### BBQ Sauce
- [ ] 2 tbsp butter
- [ ] 1 small yellow onion, finely chopped
- [ ] 3 cloves garlic, minced
- [ ] 2 cups ketchup
- [ ] 1/3 cup molasses
- [ ] 1/3 cup dark brown sugar
- [ ] 1/3 cup apple cider vinegar
- [ ] 2 tbsp yellow mustard
- [ ] 1 tbsp chili powder
- [ ] 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- [ ] 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper

### Pork
- [ ] 1 small boneless skin-off pork shoulder roast, about 2 lb
- [ ] 1 can Dr Pepper
- [ ] 1 tsp honey
- [ ] 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- [ ] 1/2 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- [ ] 2 cups Kansas City-style barbecue sauce

### Serve With
- [ ] 6 soft hamburger buns

## Instructions

### BBQ Sauce
- [ ] Melt the butter in a medium saucepan and cook the onion until softened.
- [ ] Add the garlic and cook briefly.
- [ ] Stir in the ketchup, molasses, brown sugar, vinegar, mustard, chili powder, black pepper, and cayenne.
- [ ] Bring to a boil, then simmer until slightly thickened.
- [ ] Blend the sauce until smooth and cool it to room temperature.

### Pork
- [ ] Brown the pork shoulder in a heavy pan over medium-high heat.
- [ ] Transfer the pork to a slow cooker and deglaze the pan with Dr Pepper.
- [ ] Pour the Dr Pepper over the pork, then add the honey, balsamic vinegar, salt, and pepper.
- [ ] Cook on low for 7 hours.
- [ ] Drain excess liquid and shred the meat with two forks.
- [ ] Stir in the barbecue sauce and cook for 1 more hour.
- [ ] Adjust salt and pepper to taste and serve on buns.
