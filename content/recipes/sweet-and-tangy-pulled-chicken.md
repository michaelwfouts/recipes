## Ingredients
### Sauce
- [ ] 1 1/2 cups ketchup
- [ ] 1/4 cup molasses
- [ ] 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- [ ] 1 tbsp hot sauce
- [ ] 1/2 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/2 tsp black pepper

### Chicken
- [ ] 1 cup chicken broth
- [ ] 2 tbsp molasses
- [ ] 1 tbsp sugar
- [ ] 1 tbsp liquid smoke
- [ ] 1 tsp unflavored gelatin
- [ ] Salt and pepper
- [ ] 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, halved crosswise
- [ ] Hot sauce, to taste

### Serve With
- [ ] Pickles
- [ ] Buns

## Instructions
- [ ] Whisk the sauce ingredients together in a bowl and set aside.
- [ ] Bring the broth, molasses, sugar, 2 tsp liquid smoke, gelatin, and 1 tsp salt to a boil in a Dutch oven.
- [ ] Add the chicken, return to a simmer, and cook over medium-low heat until the chicken shreds easily, about 25 minutes.
- [ ] Transfer the chicken to a bowl and set aside.
- [ ] Strain the cooking liquid through a fine-mesh strainer and skim the fat.
- [ ] Use tongs to shred the chicken into bite-size pieces.
- [ ] Return the chicken to the pot with 1 cup sauce, 1/2 cup defatted liquid, 3 tbsp reserved fat, and the remaining 1 tsp liquid smoke.
- [ ] Cook over medium heat until the liquid is absorbed and the outside of the meat looks dry, about 5 minutes.
- [ ] Season with salt, pepper, and hot sauce to taste.
- [ ] Serve with extra sauce on the side.
