## Ingredients
- [ ] 3 green tea bags
- [ ] 32 oz water
- [ ] 1 squeeze lemon juice, optional

## Instructions
- [ ] Steep the tea bags in water and place in fridge for 8 to 12 hours.
- [ ] Remove the tea bags and add a squeeze of lemon if desired.
