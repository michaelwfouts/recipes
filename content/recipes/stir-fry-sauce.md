## Ingredients
- [ ] 1/2 cup low-sodium soy sauce
- [ ] 1/3 cup chicken stock, vegetable stock, or water
- [ ] 2 tbsp brown sugar
- [ ] 2 tbsp cornstarch
- [ ] 1 tbsp sriracha, or to taste
- [ ] 4 cloves garlic, grated or minced
- [ ] 1 tbsp grated ginger

## Instructions
- [ ] Combine all ingredients in a bowl or jar.
- [ ] Whisk until smooth.
- [ ] Add to the stir-fry as needed while cooking.
