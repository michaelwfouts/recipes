## Ingredients

### Filling
- [ ] 1 tbs butter for pan
- [ ] 6 cups yams (2 40 oz cans drained)
- [ ] 3/4 cup sugar
- [ ] 2 eggs, beaten
- [ ] 1/2 cup milk
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/2 stick (1/4 cup) butter, melted
- [ ] 1/2 tsp vanilla

### Topping
- [ ] 1 cup brown sugar
- [ ] 1/3 cup flour
- [ ] 1 cup chopped pecans
- [ ] 3 tbs melted butter

## Instructions
- [ ] Preheat the oven to 350°F and butter a 9x13 dish.
- [ ] Mix the filling ingredients with mixer until smooth with a consistent texture and spread in the buttered dish.
- [ ] Mix the topping ingredients together in a small mixing bowl and sprinkle over the filling.
- [ ] Bake at 350°F for 40–45 minutes.
